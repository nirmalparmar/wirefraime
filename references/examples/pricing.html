Common questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes — upgrades apply immediately and downgrades take effect at the end of your billing cycle.

What counts as a user?

Anyone who signs in to create or edit reports. Clients viewing shared reports are always free.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Import your existing templates and start reporting the same day.

Do you offer nonprofit discounts?
